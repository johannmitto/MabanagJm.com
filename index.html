A Letter to Our Little Angel John Michael C. Mabanag We never imagined that someone so small could leave behind such a big pain. At only one year old, you left us too soon, and since the day you went away, a part of our hearts has never been whole again. You were more than just a baby—you were our happiness, our light, our little blessing. You made ordinary days feel special just by being here. And now that you are gone, everything feels heavier, quieter, and incomplete. We keep asking why your time had to be so short. Why someone so pure and innocent had to leave this world too soon. We had so many dreams for you, so many memories we still wanted to make, and so much love we still wanted to give. If love alone could have kept you here, you would have stayed with us forever. Even though we can no longer hold you, kiss you, or hear your laughter, we will carry you in our hearts every single day of our lives. You may have only been with us for one year, but your memory will stay with us for a lifetime. Our sweet little boy, you were our greatest blessing and now our deepest sorrow. Until we meet again, our angel.
